# Your Story Bank

This file is the heart of the entire behavioral interview course.

Everything from this point onward will be based on these stories.

Instead of memorizing 100 different answers, we'll build a small collection of powerful stories from your own experience
and learn how to adapt them to almost any behavioral question.

______________________________________________________________________

# What Is a Story Bank?

A Story Bank is a collection of experiences from your career that demonstrate different qualities interviewers look for.

Think of it as your personal database.

Whenever an interviewer asks,

> "Tell me about a time..."

you'll simply retrieve the most relevant story.

______________________________________________________________________

# How We'll Rate Each Story

Each story receives a strength score.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Outstanding

Excellent story that can answer many behavioral questions.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ Good

Strong story with measurable impact.

⭐⭐⭐ Average

Useful but limited.

⭐⭐ Weak

Avoid unless necessary.

# Homework (Very Important)

Before moving to the next file, write down everything you remember about these six flagship stories.

For each story, note:

- What was the business problem?
- Why was the existing solution insufficient?
- What alternatives were considered?
- What exactly was **your** contribution?
- Who else was involved?
- What technical decisions did you make?
- What challenges did you encounter?
- How did you overcome them?
- What was the measurable outcome?
- Looking back, what would you do differently?

The more specific your details, the more authentic and convincing your answers will be during interviews.
