# Interview Deep Dive

## Question

What is the biggest mistake in behavioral interviews?

### Answer

Giving generic stories without explaining your personal contribution, technical reasoning, measurable impact, and
lessons learned.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

Should I memorize answers?

### Answer

No. Memorize the structure, not the wording. Memorized answers sound unnatural. A consistent framework like STAR helps
you answer confidently while adapting to follow-up questions.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

How technical should behavioral answers be?

### Answer

For senior backend roles, include enough technical detail to demonstrate your decision-making, but avoid turning the
answer into a deep technical lecture. Balance engineering reasoning with communication and business impact.
