# Interviewer's Thought Process

Every bullet

creates

follow-up questions.

Example

Resume says

```
Built scalable

microservices

using FastAPI.
```

Interviewer thinks

- Why FastAPI?
- Why microservices?
- Why not Flask?
- How were services deployed?
- How were they monitored?
- How did they communicate?
- How did you secure them?

One bullet

can become

30 minutes

of discussion.

# Interview Deep Dive

## Question

Why do interviewers spend so much time on the resume?

### Answer

Your resume is the strongest evidence of your real-world experience. Interviewers use it to verify your depth of
knowledge, ownership, problem-solving ability, and communication skills.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

Should I list every technology I've touched?

### Answer

No. List technologies you can confidently explain in depth, including why they were chosen, how they were used, and the
trade-offs involved.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

How do I answer questions about team projects?

### Answer

Clearly separate your contributions from the team's work. Explain what you personally designed, implemented, reviewed,
or owned while acknowledging collaborative efforts.

# Practice Exercise

Take

your current resume.

For

every bullet point,

write answers

to

1. What problem did this solve?
1. Why was this approach chosen?
1. What alternatives were considered?
1. What challenges did you face?
1. What measurable impact did it have?
1. What would you improve today?

If you can answer

all six

for every bullet,

you're well prepared

for resume-based interviews.
