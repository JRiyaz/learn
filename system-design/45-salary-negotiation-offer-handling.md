> Target Audience: Senior Backend Engineers (5–10 Years)
>
> Goal: Learn how to negotiate salary, evaluate offers, handle HR conversations, negotiate remote opportunities, and maximize total compensation professionally.

# Common Interview Questions

## Why shouldn't you negotiate before an offer?

Negotiating before an offer weakens your leverage because the company has not yet decided to hire you.

______________________________________________________________________

## Should you always negotiate?

Not always. If the offer already meets or exceeds your expectations, or the company clearly states that compensation is
fixed, negotiation may not be appropriate. Evaluate the situation professionally.

______________________________________________________________________

## What is the most important compensation component?

There is no universal answer. It depends on your priorities. Evaluate the entire compensation package, career growth,
and role—not just the base salary.

# Interview Deep Dive

## Question

When is the best time to negotiate salary?

### Answer

After receiving a formal offer. At that point, the company has decided they want to hire you, giving you the strongest
negotiating position.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

Should I reveal my minimum acceptable salary?

### Answer

Generally, no. Instead, discuss market expectations or provide a reasonable range after understanding the complete role
and compensation package.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

How should I respond if the company cannot increase the base salary?

### Answer

Explore other components such as a joining bonus, equity, performance bonus, relocation support, additional vacation, or
learning budget. The overall package may still improve.

Salary negotiation is a professional skill, not a confrontation.

A successful negotiation should demonstrate

- Market awareness
- Professional communication
- Confidence without arrogance
- Honest discussion
