# Strengths & Weaknesses

> Target Audience: Software Engineers with 3–8 years of experience
>
> Goal: Learn how to answer questions about your strengths and weaknesses honestly, professionally, and in a way that demonstrates self-awareness and continuous growth.

______________________________________________________________________

# Introduction

Almost every interview includes these questions.

```
What are your strengths?

↓

What are your weaknesses?
```

Many candidates either

- sound arrogant, or
- sound unprepared.

Neither creates a good impression.

______________________________________________________________________

# What Interviewers Are Actually Evaluating

These questions are **not** about finding perfect people.

Interviewers want to know

- Are you self-aware?
- Can you evaluate yourself objectively?
- Do you actively improve?
- Are your strengths useful?
- Are your weaknesses manageable?

______________________________________________________________________

# The Biggest Mistake

Candidates think

```

Impress Interviewer

```

## Problem Solving

> One of my strengths is problem-solving. I enjoy breaking complex problems into smaller parts and identifying practical solutions. For example, in one project we experienced slow API responses during peak usage. After analyzing database queries and application logs, I helped optimize the queries and introduced pagination, which significantly improved response times.

______________________________________________________________________

Why it works

Shows

- Technical ability
- Ownership
- Results

## Delegation

# The Improvement Principle

Every weakness

must end

like this.

```
Weakness

↓

Action Taken

↓

Improvement

↓

Still Learning
```

Interviewers love

continuous improvement.

______________________________________________________________________

# Strong Example

Question

"What is your biggest weakness?"

Answer

> Earlier in my career I found it difficult to delegate work because I preferred solving technical problems myself. Over time I realized that effective engineering is about enabling the team, not just individual contribution. I've been working on improving task delegation, conducting knowledge-sharing sessions, and involving teammates earlier during implementation. It's something I continue to improve.

Notice

The answer

ends positively.

# Interview Deep Dive

## Question

Should I choose a technical weakness?

### Answer

Only if it doesn't directly prevent you from performing the role and you're actively improving it. In many cases,
communication, delegation, or presentation skills are safer choices because they show self-awareness without raising
concerns about core technical competence.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

Can I say "perfectionism" is my weakness?

### Answer

Yes, but only if you explain how it affected your work, what you learned, and how you've adjusted your approach.
Otherwise, it often sounds like a memorized interview answer.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

How many strengths and weaknesses should I mention?

### Answer

One or two of each is usually enough. It's better to explain them well with examples than to list many without depth.
