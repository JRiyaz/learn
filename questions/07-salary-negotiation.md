# Salary Negotiation & HR Closing Round

> Target Audience: Software Engineers with 3–8 years of experience
>
> Goal: Learn how to negotiate salary professionally, maximize your offer, and avoid common mistakes during the HR closing round.

______________________________________________________________________

# Introduction

Many engineers spend months preparing for

- DSA
- System Design
- Technical Interviews

Then lose

₹5–20 Lakhs

(or thousands of euros)

because they don't know

how to negotiate.

Salary negotiation

is a skill.

______________________________________________________________________

# The HR Closing Round

A typical HR closing discussion

looks like

```
Current Salary

↓

Expected Salary

↓

Notice Period

↓

Offer Discussion

↓

Negotiation

↓

Final Offer
```

______________________________________________________________________

# What HR Is Evaluating

HR wants to know

- Can we afford you?
- Are your expectations realistic?
- Will you accept the offer?
- Are you interviewing elsewhere?
- Are there any risks?

______________________________________________________________________

# The Biggest Mistake

Candidate

answers immediately.

```
Expected Salary?

↓

40 LPA.
```

No discussion.

No reasoning.

No negotiation.

______________________________________________________________________

# Principle 1

## Never Be the First to Commit

If possible,

understand

their range first.

Example

> Before I answer, could you share the budgeted range for this position?

Sometimes

they'll tell you.

Sometimes

they won't.

Worth asking.

______________________________________________________________________

# Question 1

## What is your current salary?

______________________________________________________________________

### Why They Ask

To understand

your compensation history.

______________________________________________________________________

### Best Answer

Always answer honestly.

Never inflate numbers.

If your compensation includes

- Bonus
- Variable Pay
- Stocks

clarify that.

Example

> My current total compensation consists of fixed salary along with an annual performance bonus.

______________________________________________________________________

### Don't Do This

Never lie.

Background verification

or documentation

may expose it.

______________________________________________________________________

# Question 2

## What are your salary expectations?

______________________________________________________________________

### Weak Answer

> Anything is fine.

Problem

Looks unprepared.

______________________________________________________________________

### Another Weak Answer

> 1 Crore.

Without justification.

______________________________________________________________________

### Better Answer

> Based on my experience, technical skills, and the responsibilities of this role, I'm looking for a package in the range of ₹XX–YY LPA. Of course, I'm also considering the overall opportunity, growth, and responsibilities.

______________________________________________________________________

Notice

Range

instead of

single number.

______________________________________________________________________

# Why Give A Range?


# If HR Says

## That's Too High

Don't panic.

Good response

> I'm certainly open to discussion. My expectation is based on the responsibilities of the role, my experience, and current market conditions. I'd be happy to understand the overall compensation structure before making a final decision.

Professional.

Flexible.

______________________________________________________________________

# If HR Asks

## What's Your Minimum?

Avoid

giving

your absolute minimum.

Better

> I'd prefer to evaluate the complete offer, including compensation, responsibilities, growth opportunities, and benefits before deciding.

______________________________________________________________________

# Current Offer Question

## Do You Have Other Offers?

Always answer

truthfully.

Example

> I'm currently interviewing with a few organizations, but I'm evaluating opportunities based on both technical growth and overall fit rather than compensation alone.

______________________________________________________________________

# Never Create Fake Offers

HR professionals

have seen

this

many times.

If discovered,

credibility

is lost.

______________________________________________________________________

# Counter Offer

Question

```
What if your current employer

matches the offer?
```

Weak Answer

> I'll stay.

HR

may stop negotiating.

______________________________________________________________________

Better

> While compensation is important, I'm primarily looking for long-term technical growth and new challenges. My decision will be based on the overall opportunity rather than salary alone.

______________________________________________________________________

# Notice Period

Question

"When can you join?"

Answer honestly.

If negotiable,

mention it.

Never promise

a date

you cannot meet.

______________________________________________________________________

# Joining Bonus

Many candidates

never ask.

You should.

Especially if

you lose

annual bonus,

stock,

or incentives.

Example

> Since I'll be forfeiting my annual bonus by changing jobs now, would there be any possibility of a joining bonus?

Professional.

Reasonable.

______________________________________________________________________

# Interview Deep Dive

## Question

Should I negotiate salary?

### Answer

Yes. Most companies expect reasonable negotiation, especially for experienced engineers. Negotiation should be based on
your experience, market value, and the responsibilities of the role, not emotion.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

Should I reveal my minimum salary?

### Answer

Generally, no. It's better to discuss your expected range and evaluate the complete compensation package before
committing to a minimum.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

How many times should I negotiate?

### Answer

Usually one or two professional discussions are enough. Repeated negotiations over small amounts can create a negative
impression.

# Summary

Salary negotiation is not about demanding more money.

It's about communicating your value professionally.

Strong candidates

- Know their market value.
- Stay flexible.
- Evaluate the complete opportunity.
- Negotiate respectfully.
- Think long-term.

Remember

The goal is not simply to maximize your first salary,

but to make the best long-term career decision.
