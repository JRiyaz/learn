# Resume Walkthrough

> Target Audience: Software Engineers with 3–8 years of experience
>
> Goal: Learn how to confidently explain every section of your resume and handle deep follow-up questions.

______________________________________________________________________

# Why Resume Walkthrough Matters

Many experienced candidates believe

```
Technical Interview

↓

Coding

↓

System Design
```

In reality,

many interviewers spend

30–45 minutes

discussing only your resume.

If you cannot confidently explain your own experience,

the interviewer will immediately lose confidence.

______________________________________________________________________

# What Interviewers Are Actually Evaluating

They're not simply reading your resume.

They're asking

```
Did you actually do this work?
```

They're evaluating

- Technical depth
- Ownership
- Communication
- Decision making
- Honesty
- Problem-solving
- Impact

______________________________________________________________________

# The Resume Pyramid

Every line on your resume

should support

this structure.

```
Experience

↓

Responsibilities

↓

Technical Decisions

↓

Challenges

↓

Business Impact
```

Never stop at

"I used FastAPI."

Explain

why,

how,

and what changed.

______________________________________________________________________

# The Resume Rule

A simple rule.

If you write

one line

on your resume,

expect

10–20 questions.

Example

Resume

```
Built REST APIs using FastAPI.
```

Possible questions

- Why FastAPI?
- Why not Flask?
- How was authentication implemented?
- How were APIs versioned?
- How was validation handled?
- How was testing done?
- How was deployment done?
- What problems did you face?
- How many requests per second?
- What would you improve?

One line.

Ten questions.

# Example

Question

"What does your company do?"

Weak answer

> We build software.

Good answer

> Our company develops enterprise SaaS solutions for financial institutions. My team focuses on backend services responsible for user management and transaction processing.

Specific.

Professional.

My responsibilities included designing REST APIs, implementing authentication, integrating RabbitMQ for asynchronous processing, and deploying containerized services to AWS. ✅ Know every line on your resume.

✅ Explain impact, not just implementation.

✅ Use real examples.

✅ Distinguish personal contributions from team work.

✅ Be honest about your level of involvement.

# Interview Deep Dive

## Question

Why do interviewers spend so much time on resumes?

### Answer

Your resume represents your real-world engineering experience. Interviewers use it to verify technical depth, ownership,
communication skills, and the authenticity of your claimed experience.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

What is the biggest mistake candidates make during resume discussions?

### Answer

Listing technologies without being able to explain how they were used, why they were chosen, what problems they solved,
and what their own contribution was.

______________________________________________________________________

## Question

How should you describe a project?

### Answer

Explain the business problem, architecture, your responsibilities, technical challenges, solutions you implemented, and
the measurable impact of your work.

# Practice Exercise

Take your resume.

For **every single bullet point**,

write answers to these questions.

1. Why was this needed?
1. What exactly did I do?
1. What technologies were involved?
1. What challenges did I face?
1. What alternatives did we consider?
1. What was the business impact?
1. What would I improve today?

If you cannot answer all seven,

that bullet needs more preparation.
