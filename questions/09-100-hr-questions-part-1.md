Don't memorize answers.

Instead,

for every question,

prepare a real experience from your career.

A good answer should demonstrate

- Ownership
- Communication
- Decision Making
- Learning
- Professionalism

# Self-Evaluation Checklist

After preparing your answers,

ask yourself

```
Did I answer

confidently?

↓

Did I give

a real example?

↓

Did I explain

the impact?

↓

Did I show

ownership?

↓

Did I mention

what I learned?
```

If the answer is

**Yes**

you're on the right track.

# Practice Strategy

Don't prepare

100 different stories.

Instead,

prepare

10–15 strong stories

covering

- Success
- Failure
- Leadership
- Conflict
- Production Issue
- Learning
- Mentoring
- Process Improvement
- Difficult Decision
- Customer Impact

You'll discover that

the same stories

can answer

dozens

of HR questions.
