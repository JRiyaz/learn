# 14. Common Interview Traps

## Trap 1

Starting architecture immediately.

Wrong.

Understand the problem first.

______________________________________________________________________

## Trap 2

Assuming requirements.

Always clarify.

______________________________________________________________________

## Trap 3

Trying to design everything.

Reduce scope.

______________________________________________________________________

## Trap 4

Ignoring scale.

A system for

1,000 users

and

100 million users

is completely different.

______________________________________________________________________

## Trap 5

Ignoring Non-Functional Requirements.

Many candidates only discuss features.

Senior engineers discuss:

- Availability
- Reliability
- Consistency
- Scalability

______________________________________________________________________

## Trap 6

Never confirming assumptions.

Example:

> I'll assume the system supports approximately 10 million daily active users. Please let me know if you'd like me to design for a different scale.

This is a strong interview habit.

# 15. Frequently Asked Follow-up Questions

### Why did you choose this scope?

Because it covers the core business functionality within the interview time.

______________________________________________________________________

### Why ignore other features?

To prioritize the most important requirements first.

______________________________________________________________________

### Why ask so many questions?

To avoid incorrect assumptions and understand the business problem.

______________________________________________________________________

### How long should requirement gathering take?

Typically

5–10 minutes

depending on interview duration.

______________________________________________________________________

### What if the interviewer says "Make reasonable assumptions"?

Clearly state your assumptions before proceeding.

______________________________________________________________________

### Should I estimate capacity before requirements?

No.

Understand the problem first.

______________________________________________________________________

### Should APIs be discussed now?

Only at a high level.

Detailed API design comes later.

## Golden Rules

✅ Never start drawing architecture immediately.

✅ Ask questions before making assumptions.

✅ Limit the scope.

✅ Prioritize must-have features.

✅ Discuss both functional and non-functional requirements.

✅ State assumptions explicitly.

✅ Confirm the scope before moving to capacity estimation.

✅ Think like a product owner before thinking like an engineer.

# 18. Final Notes

Requirement gathering is not a formality—it is one of the strongest signals of senior engineering maturity.

Junior engineers often jump straight to technology choices.

Senior engineers first understand:

- The business problem
- The users
- The scale
- The constraints
- The trade-offs

Only then do they begin designing the system.

A great architecture built for the wrong problem is still the wrong solution.
