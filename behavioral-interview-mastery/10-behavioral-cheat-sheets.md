# Questions To Ask Interviewers

Always prepare

3–5 questions.

Examples

- How is technical ownership distributed across the team?
- What challenges is the engineering team currently solving?
- How do engineers grow into Senior or Staff roles?
- What does success look like during the first six months?
- How are architecture decisions made?

Good questions

leave

a lasting impression.

# Final Advice

Interviewers

are not searching

for perfect engineers.

They're searching for engineers who

- Solve problems
- Learn continuously
- Communicate clearly
- Take ownership
- Work well with others
- Improve the business

Focus on demonstrating those qualities,
